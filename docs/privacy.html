Wipe

Privacy Policy — Last updated: June 2026

Summary

Wipe does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data Collection

Wipe does not collect any data. Specifically:

• We do not log or transmit keystrokes, typed text, or any input you enter using the keyboard.
• We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location.
• We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
• We do not share any data with third parties.

Full Access

Wipe requests "Allow Full Access" in iOS keyboard settings. This permission is required to save your selected theme and preferences across the app and keyboard extension using a shared storage container on your device. No data leaves your device as a result of granting this permission.

Local Storage

Wipe stores the following data locally on your device only:

• Your selected keyboard theme (e.g. System, Sakura, Wood).
• No other data is stored.

Children

Wipe does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.

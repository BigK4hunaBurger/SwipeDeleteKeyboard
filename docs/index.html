Wipe

Delete smarter. Type faster.

Features

  • ⌫

    Swipe to Delete

    Press and hold backspace, then slide left to select a range of text. Release to erase it all at once — no more tapping dozens of times.

  • あ

    Japanese IME

    Full 12-key flick input with real-time kanji conversion powered by AzooKey. Tap a candidate to confirm. Remaining characters stay in the bar for continued editing.

  • A

    English QWERTY

    Clean QWERTY layout with autocomplete. Double-tap Shift for Caps Lock. One letter shifts back automatically.

  • ↔

    Cursor Control

    Slide left or right on the Space key to move the cursor precisely, one character at a time.

  • ↩

    Undo

    Tap ⤺ to undo your last input or deletion, one step at a time.

10 Themes

Long-press ⤺ to cycle themes without leaving the keyboard.

System Terminal Neon Sakura Midnight Paper Sunset Wood Metal Ice

How to Enable

  1. 1

    Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards

  2. 2

    Tap Add New Keyboard and select Wipe

  3. 3

    Tap Allow Full Access to enable theme sync between the app and keyboard

日本語

Wipe は⌫を押しながら左スライドで範囲削除できる iOS カスタムキーボードです。 日本語フリック入力（ひらがな・漢字変換）と英語 QWERTY に対応。 テーマは10種類。⤺ を長押しでキーボード内から切り替えられます。

設定 → 一般 → キーボード → キーボードを追加 → Wipe
